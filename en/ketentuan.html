House rules

So every guest can rest peacefully and safely.

01

Check-in and check-out

  • Check-in is from 2:00 PM WIB and check-out is by 12:00 noon WIB.
  • Early check-in requests must be confirmed with the management in advance.
  • Late check-out is charged IDR 100,000 per hour.
02

Payment and deposit

  • The minimum accommodation deposit is IDR 150,000 per tent booked.
  • Activities: rafting and ATV can be paid on site. Paintball requires a deposit of at least 50%, a minimum of 10 players, and booking at least 3 days in advance.
  • The balance is due before check-out.
  • Payment is by bank transfer to the account our admin sends when confirming your booking.
  • Deposits are non-refundable.
03

Safety, privacy and facilities

  • Additional guests must be reported to the management. Outside visitors are allowed until 10:00 PM WIB.
  • Speakers and amplified sound are allowed until 10:00 PM WIB.
  • Bladed weapons, drugs and other prohibited items are not allowed.
  • Any indecent or unlawful behaviour will be reported to the authorities.
  • Please use the facilities with care; guests are responsible for any intentional damage.
  • The management is not responsible for lost personal belongings.
