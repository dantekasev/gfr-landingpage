About us

Our journey
01

Before

Years in agribusiness

Before Green Forest Riverside became the place that has now welcomed more than 200 families, groups of friends and colleagues, there was a long journey behind it that was not always smooth.

We did not start here. For years we tried to build a business in agribusiness, and we failed more than once. Plans stalled halfway, money ran out before results appeared, and there were nights spent wondering whether we had chosen the right path. But through all those failures, there was one thing we never lost: a plot of land by the river that always felt like it held something we had not yet made the most of.

02

Turning point

From agribusiness to agritourism

One day, we stopped seeing the river as a backdrop and started seeing it as the destination. The sound of the water, the cool shade of the trees around it, the open space so rarely found in everyday city life. All of it had a value we had overlooked. So we decided to change course completely: from agribusiness to agritourism. It was not an easy decision, because it meant rebuilding from scratch with an entirely new way of seeing things.

One day, we stopped seeing the river as a backdrop and started seeing it as the destination.

03

1 April 2025

Six months of building, then opening day

We spent the next six months making it happen. Designing, building, fixing and rebuilding every corner we felt would matter to the people who came. And on 1 April 2025, Green Forest Riverside officially unzipped its tents for the very first time.

04

The first year

More than 200 bookings

We did not stop at opening day. In our first year, we were trusted with more than 200 bookings, from family gatherings looking for quality time together to company outings needing a break from targets and deadlines. Every booking is a reminder that our bold decision to change course has brought us to the place where we belong.

05

Today

Exclusive cabin under construction

We are still on our way to real stability, and we do not hide it. That is exactly what makes this story honest. We are growing step by step, learning from every guest who comes, and always improving. Right now we are building an exclusive cabin, one more step towards a warmer, more personal experience for anyone who wants to stay and truly become part of the river and forest around us.

Green Forest Riverside is not a story of instant success. It is a story of how many times we fell before finding a place that truly feels like home. And now, we want to share that home with you.

From the river we begin.
