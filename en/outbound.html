Good to know

Before you book.

Payment

Rafting and ATV can be paid on site. Paintball requires a deposit of at least 50% when booking, with the balance paid on site. Deposits are non-refundable.

Weather

Activity schedules may change depending on the weather and river conditions. We will keep you updated.

Groups & gatherings

We host groups with any combination of camping, rafting, ATV and paintball. Send us your estimated group size and dates, and we will prepare an attractive package offer.
