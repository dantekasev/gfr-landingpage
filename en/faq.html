Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions guests ask most.

Do I need to bring my own camping gear?

No. Tents are already set up with mattresses, sleeping bags/blankets, pillows, a table, chairs, a grill set, an electric kettle and a power outlet. Just bring a change of clothes, toiletries and any personal medication. We strongly recommend bringing your own food and cooking ingredients.

What if it rains?

Every tent stands on a wooden deck under a roof, so the seating area can still be used in light rain. For activities, the schedule may change depending on the weather and river conditions. We will keep you updated.

Can you host company outings or large groups?

Yes. We host groups with any combination of camping, rafting, ATV and paintball. Send us your estimated group size and dates, and we will prepare a package offer.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM WIB and check-out is by 12:00 noon WIB. See the house rules for details.

How much is the deposit and how do I pay?

The accommodation deposit is IDR 150,000 per tent booked. For activities, rafting and ATV can be paid on site, while paintball requires a deposit of at least 50%. Payment is by bank transfer to the account our admin sends when confirming your booking. The balance is due before check-out. Deposits are non-refundable.

How many people fit in one tent?

One tent fits 4–6 people, with breakfast included for 4. From the 5th guest onwards, breakfast is IDR 15,000 per person, and extra beds are available.

Can we come by bus or minibus?

Yes. The site is reachable by private car; if you are coming by bus or minibus, let us know first so we can direct you to the right parking spot. See location & directions.

How far is it from the parking area to the tents?

Close by. Just 150–200 metres, with views of the pine forest. For luggage and guests who need a lift, our on-site team is ready with motorbikes.

Still have questions?

Our admin is happy to help on WhatsApp. Include your planned dates and we will reply with availability and the total cost.
