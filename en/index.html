Green Forest Riverside

Right between the river and the pine forest.

Set on the banks of the Palayangan River, the only rafting route in Pangalengan, Bandung.

The river

Every unit faces the river directly and has its own wooden deck for relaxing or spending time together.

Pine forest

Quiet surroundings and the fresh air of the Bandung highlands.

Hassle-free

Tents are already set up, everything you need is prepared, and our on-site team is on standby 24 hours.

Together

Great for family gatherings, friends, company outings and school groups.

Explore

Rafting, ATV, paintball, or simply a relaxed walk through the pine forest, along the river or around the tea plantation.
