Riverside camping

Tents for 4–6 people on a private wooden deck, complete with a table, chairs and a grill set.

Camping area

river-facing views.

Every unit has its own area and deck facing the river, a private space to relax and spend time together, separate from other guests.

Each tent stands on a wooden platform with its own roof. The stove, gas, grill set and drinking water come at no extra cost. Clean bathrooms are right behind the tent area.
